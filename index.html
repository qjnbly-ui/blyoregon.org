Welcome to Bly

Bly is a small community on Oregon Route 140 in Klamath County. Known for its scenic landscapes, history, and warm small-town character, Bly is both a peaceful home and a gateway to outdoor recreation.

Surrounded by forests, trails, and sites of cultural significance, Bly brings local life, natural beauty, and community action together.

Featured Places

Bly Ranger Station

Historic Forest Service station built by the Civilian Conservation Corps — now on the National Register of Historic Places.

Mitchell Recreation Area

Memorial & picnic site marking WWII history on the mainland U.S.

Recreation Highlights

OC&E Woods Line Trail

Explore miles of trail for hiking, biking, and seeking the high desert horizon.

Gearhart Mountain Wilderness

Relax, fish, picnic, and enjoy quiet days near water and pines.
