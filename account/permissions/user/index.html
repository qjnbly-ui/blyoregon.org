Access settings

Use categories below to manage the member's profile settings, messaging identity, alerts, article workflow, media tools, and bucket-specific access.

Member

Account role

Choose the overall access level for this member. Use moderator as the semi-admin layer, then add the exact tool permissions below.

Member and moderator roles do not automatically turn on tool access. Use the permission toggles below to grant the exact powers you want.

Messaging identity

Controls how this member is identified inside direct-message conversations. This is separate from when alerts are sent.

Show name in messages When off, this member appears as “Bly member” inside message threads.

Alert settings

These are the same member-controlled alert settings shown in profile settings during account creation and later in the account editor.

Article submissions: inbox Updates when one of this member's article drafts moves into or through review.
Article submissions: email Email updates when article drafts move into or through review.
Article review: inbox Changes requested and other review decisions on this member's articles.
Article review: email Email changes requested and other review decisions.
Article publishing: inbox Publishing, live updates, and unpublishing events tied to this member's articles.
Article publishing: email Email publishing, live update, and unpublishing events.
Message alerts: inbox New messages from other members and authors on the site.
Message alerts: email Email alerts for new member-to-member messages.
Business updates: inbox Claim decisions, review changes, publishing changes, and other updates tied to this member's business listings.
Business updates: email Email alerts for business listing workflow changes.
Admin alerts: inbox Reviewer/admin alerts when submissions need attention or admin-side publishing actions happen.
Admin alerts: email Email alerts for reviewer/admin article work.
Business admin alerts: inbox Reviewer/admin alerts when business submissions or ownership claims need attention.
Business admin alerts: email Email alerts for business review queue work.

Media permissions

Controls uploads, the media manager, and photo-level actions inside managed buckets.

Photo uploads Allows this member to submit photos from the site tools.
Media manager Gives access to the media manager and unlocks the bucket permissions below.
Edit photo details Allows editing titles, captions, stories, tagged people, and other archive metadata.
Rename media Reserved for future rename tools in the media manager.
Delete media Controls whether this member can remove files from managed buckets.

Article permissions

Controls writing, review, and publishing for the dynamic user-submitted article workflow.

Submit articles Lets this member create drafts and submit user articles for review.
Publish own articles Lets this member publish new articles without admin review.
Publish own article edits Lets this member update already-approved articles without sending edits back through review.
Review articles Lets this member review submitted articles and request changes.
Publish articles Lets this member approve and publish user-submitted articles.

Bucket access

Choose which historical photo buckets this member can work in when media manager access is enabled.

Danger zone
