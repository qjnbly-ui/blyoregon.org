Klamath Echoes is a series of books written by volunteers of the Klamath County Historical Society to preserve the history of the Klamath Basin, Southern Oregon, and Northern California. The series started in 1964 and was published annually until 1978, totaling 16 volumes. This issue focuses on Sprague River and Bly.
