Ask Bly

An in-development AI storyteller built from the pages of this site. It speaks as the town and shares what our archives know.

How it works

A guide built from our pages

  • Answers come from the stories, interviews, and archives on blyoregon.org.
  • Ask about history, places, and community resources currently listed.
  • It is still learning, so if something sounds off, please tell us.
Tips

Get a better answer

  • Use names, dates, or places for more precise results.
  • For services or visits, ask directly about businesses or lodging.
  • Say "sources" if you want to see what page it used.
