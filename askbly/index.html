Behind the scenes

How Ask Bly works

Ask Bly is built from the pages on this site. We regularly scan the public HTML, extract the text, and store it as a searchable library. When you ask a question, the system compares your words to that library and pulls the most relevant passages.

A language model then turns those passages into a short, friendly answer. It is instructed to stay grounded in the retrieved text and avoid adding facts that are not explicitly in the site content. If a detail is missing, it should say so and ask a brief follow-up question.

When to use it

Best questions

  • Local history, people, and places in Bly.
  • Named landmarks, events, and timelines.
  • Services, lodging, or places to visit.

For practical questions, Ask Bly prioritizes the Business Directory. It only includes contact details when asked.
